# Victron and related primary documentation

Checked 2026-10-01. Reopen the exact model's current manual before applying installation values. These are documentation checks, not default design settings.

- [SmartSolar 150/70–250/100 VE.Can specifications](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Manual_SmartSolar_MPPT_150-70_up_to_250-100_VE.Can/en/technical-specifications.html): distinguish PV maximum voltage from battery charge current. The 250/70's PV limit is 250 V absolute in the coldest conditions, 245 V during startup/operation. Check the actual string's cold Voc and input current; panel watts alone do not settle compatibility.
- [Cerbo GX connections](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Cerbo_GX/en/connecting-victron-products.html) and [hardware overview](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Cerbo_GX/en/installation.html): verify GX generation and the selected CAN port's isolation/profile. Document VE.Bus, VE.Direct and CAN separately, including termination. Different-rate battery and Victron CAN devices must use separate compatible buses. RJ45 is a connector shape, not an Ethernet guarantee.
- [Victron battery compatibility](https://www.victronenergy.com/live/battery_compatibility%3Astart): an absent battery listing means compatibility remains unverified and needs supplier evidence; it is not by itself proof of incompatibility. Obtain the actual battery's chemistry, charging limits, addressing, cable pinout and termination. Never generalize a battery's master ID or CAN profile to all brands.
- [Wiring Unlimited: DC wiring](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/The_Wiring_Unlimited_book/en/dc-wiring.html): lithium fuse selection needs prospective fault current and interrupt rating as well as amperage. Record those facts with protection devices; do not reuse a photographed fuse value as a calculated recommendation.
- [Wiring Unlimited: ground/earth](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/The_Wiring_Unlimited_book/en/ground,-earth-and-electrical-safety.html) and [MultiPlus-II 120 V installation](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/MultiPlus-II_120V/en/installation.html): separate DC return, chassis/PE and AC neutral. Show grounding/bonding behavior in external-source and inverter modes according to the exact model and installation. Do not automatically add a permanent neutral/PE bond.
- [MultiPlus-II product variants](https://www.victronenergy.com/inverters-chargers/multiplus-ii): a partial label such as `48/5000/70` omits information needed to distinguish AC variants. Verify the complete SKU and input/output terminal plan before drawing a generator or shore supply connection.
- [ICT200DB-12IRC manual, rev 2.01](https://ict-power.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/855-314-002-Instruction-Manual-ICT200DB-12IRC-Rev2.01.pdf), sections 2.12/2.14: output returns are common. Mixed-polarity sources require electrical isolation. Check the actual DC-DC converter and reference scheme before combining negative telecom and positive low-voltage buses. Documentation must name OUT/RTN and chassis connections distinctly.
- [Leviton L5-30P](https://leviton.com/products/2611), [L14-30R](https://leviton.com/products/2710) and [terminal diagrams](https://leviton.com/content/dam/leviton/commercial-industrial/product_documents/instruction_sheet/PK-93786-10-00-0A.pdf): L5-30 uses three wires at 125 V; L14-30 uses four at 125/250 V. Identify P/R/inlet/outlet, all cores and any verified adapter explicitly; do not join similarly named pictures and assume compatibility.
- [GenStart EM-S manufacturer listing](https://www.genstart.ca/start-systems.html): verify the exact generator generation and start harness; the listing excludes the My Generator app/Bluetooth models. A generic two-wire-start claim does not verify the actual harness pinout, relay state or feedback.

If the user supplies a matching manufacturer manual, record its revision and distinguish instructions applicable to that model from the user's request. Resolve conflicting editions by checking the hardware and current manufacturer source; never silently select a convenient rating.
