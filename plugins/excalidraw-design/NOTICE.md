# Asset provenance

Equipment symbols, network inventory, diagrams and skill instructions are original project contributions distributed under the included MIT license. They are editable generic illustrations, not manufacturer logos or copied official icon sets. Hardware names identify example products and do not imply endorsement.

Electrical instructions retain general visual conventions learned from an author-supplied diagram. The shared package contains no original customer scene, product raster images or private project facts. Its editable electrical equipment is generic original artwork; the fictional wiring inventory documents disconnected examples and does not specify a complete installation or verified pinouts. Manufacturer manuals are linked as references and are not redistributed.

Excalifont is rendered by the editor; this package redistributes no font binary. The included advance metrics support wrapping. Manufacturer sources and alias assumptions are listed in the network skill reference.
